Now in public beta for kitchens of up to 30 staff

Shift rotas for small kitchens, planned in minutes

Rotaleaf lays out a week of shifts on one screen, totals everyone's hours as you go, and sends each person their shifts by text when you publish.

Features

  • Planner

    One-screen week

    Drag shifts onto a week view and copy last week's pattern in one step.

  • Phones

    Swap requests

    Staff ask to swap from their phone. You approve or decline, and the rota updates for everyone.

  • Totals

    Hours as you plan

    Planned hours sit beside contracted hours for each person, so overtime shows before you publish.

  • Sharing

    Text, not another app

    Published shifts go out by text message. Nobody needs to install anything to see their week.

  • Export

    Payroll export

    Download approved hours as a CSV with one row per person per shift.

  • Offline

    Works offline

    Plan on a tablet in the back office with no signal. Changes sync when you reconnect.

Questions

What does it cost during the beta?

Nothing. Before the beta ends we will publish pricing at least 30 days ahead of any charge, and you can export your rotas at any time.

Does it replace our payroll system?

No. Rotaleaf plans shifts and totals hours. It exports a CSV that you can import into your payroll provider.

Do my staff need smartphones?

No. Shifts and swap confirmations arrive by text message, which works on any phone.
