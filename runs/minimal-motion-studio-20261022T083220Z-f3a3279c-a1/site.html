What we do

  • 01

    Garden survey

    A half-day visit to measure light, soil and drainage, followed by a written summary within a week.

    Half day

  • 02

    Planting plan

    A scaled plan with a plant list, sourcing notes and a first-year care calendar.

    2 to 3 weeks

  • 03

    Build oversight

    We brief the landscaper you choose and check the work at each stage, so the plan survives the build.

    Per project

Questions

Do you build the gardens yourselves?

No. We design and oversee. We can recommend landscapers we have worked with, or brief one you already trust.

How far do you travel?

Up to about an hour from the studio. Further visits are quoted separately before anything is booked.

What does a survey cost?

A fixed fee, agreed before the visit. If you go on to commission a planting plan, the survey fee is taken off it.
