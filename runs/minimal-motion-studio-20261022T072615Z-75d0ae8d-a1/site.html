Questions

Can I order large quantities?

Yes, with 48 hours' notice for up to 20 loaves. Send the date and what you need, and we will confirm by email.

Is anything gluten free?

Our kitchen uses wheat flour all day, so we cannot promise anything is free of gluten. The almond cake contains no wheat flour.

Do you deliver?

Not yet. Orders are for collection from the shop during opening hours.
